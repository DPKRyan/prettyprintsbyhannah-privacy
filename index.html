JARVIS privacy notice

16 October 2026

JARVIS is an internal tool operated by Pretty Prints by Hannah. Its Pinterest marketing feature is currently in development and is intended only for our own business account.

Purpose and information used

Once authorised, the feature will prepare marketing content from our Etsy product listings and approved images. Each Pin must be explicitly approved before scheduled publication.

The integration may access our Pinterest account identifiers, boards, Pins and supported performance metrics. Authorisation tokens will be used to connect through Pinterest’s official authentication process; JARVIS will not request Pinterest passwords.

This feature is not intended to process Etsy buyers’ names, addresses, messages or personalisation details.

Use, sharing and retention

Information will be used only to prepare approved content, publish and verify Pins, and measure our own marketing performance. Pinterest API information will not be sold, shared with AI providers or used to train AI models.

Pinterest information will be retrieved and retained only as permitted by Pinterest’s terms. Credentials will be kept private and excluded from public website files and source repositories.

Control and contact

Access can be revoked through Pinterest’s account settings. For questions or requests concerning access, correction or deletion of information handled by this feature, contact:

prettyprintsbyhannah@hotmail.com
